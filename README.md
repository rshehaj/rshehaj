### 🔥 What I'm building

#### 💇 [Reserve](https://reserve.al): an AI receptionist for hair salons
A **chat receptionist** that books appointments in Albanian and English, around the clock, **without ever double-booking**. Now in a pilot with a real salon in Tirana.

- 🧠 **The LLM only talks**: Claude handles the conversation through tools, while a deterministic slot engine decides availability and a Postgres exclusion constraint is the final guard against overlaps
- 🧪 **Scripted evaluation suite** of Albanian and English conversations, plus property-based tests, end-to-end tests and k6 load tests in CI
- 📅 **Owner dashboard** with a live agenda, manual bookings, customer history and handoff when a chat needs a human
- 📱 **Stylist phone app (PWA)**, booking emails, and web push for reminders, salon replies and new bookings
- 🚢 **Every green push to `main` deploys** to production, with automatic rollback

`Java 21` · `Spring Boot` · `PostgreSQL` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Claude API` · `Docker`

#### 📚 [maturanteve.al](https://maturanteve.al): exam prep for Albanian students
A live platform that helps high-school students get ready for the **State Matura** and the **mini-matura**. I designed, built, and run it end to end, solo.

- ✍ **Practice tests and mock exams** built from real past exam papers, with instant grading and explanations
- 📊 **Progress analytics** that show students exactly where they're strong and what to work on next, plus rankings and score predictions
- 🧑‍🏫 **AI exam builder for teachers** that generates complete, curriculum-aligned tests in minutes
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Parent digests** that keep families in the loop on their child's progress
- 💳 **Card payments, a referral program, email campaigns and web push notifications**, all built in-house

`Laravel` · `Vue` · `MySQL` · `Redis` · `Docker` · `OpenAI API`

#### 🏛 [Apollonia](https://github.com/rshehaj/apollonia): an open-source AI tutor
An **open-source AI tutor** for the Albanian State Matura exam. Students ask questions in Albanian, and Apollonia answers **only from retrieved, openly licensed sources**, citing the exact source revision every time. No hallucinated history lessons! 🙅

- 🔎 Hybrid search (`bge-m3` vectors + accent-folded keyword search, fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion) over a curated Albanian Wikipedia corpus
- 🧪 Retrieval evaluation on 50 labeled Albanian questions: **Recall@5 of 0.98–1.00**, with every question's source in the top 10
- ✅ `v0.1.0` released, with `mypy --strict`, integration tests against real Postgres + pgvector, and CI on every change
- 🗺 Roadmap: streaming cited answers → practice quizzes → open-answer checking → progress tracking

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `pgvector` · `RAG` · `Hybrid search` · `MIT licensed` 💚

#### 🌐 [intellycode.al](https://intellycode.al): my studio's website
A fast, **trilingual** (English, Italian, Albanian) static site with a live demo terminal that replays real project transcripts, and it works without JavaScript too.

`Astro` · `TypeScript` · `nginx` · `Docker`
